How DPIP Works

📋

1. Government Posts Tender

Government officials (admins) create and publish tenders for public infrastructure projects with full details and budget estimates.

🏦

2. Contractors Bid

Registered contractors review tender details and submit competitive bids before the deadline. All bids are recorded transparently.

🏆

3. Highest Bid Wins

After the deadline, the platform allocates the contract to the highest bidder. The government confirms the award with full transparency.

🌐

4. Publicly Displayed

All awarded contracts, bid histories, and project timelines are publicly accessible on the tracker — full accountability for every rupee spent.
